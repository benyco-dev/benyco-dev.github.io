## Common questions

**I changed phones. How do I get my purchases back?**
Open the app, go to **Premium** (or the Shop) and tap **Restore purchases**. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
Open **Settings → your name → Subscriptions** on your iPhone and choose *I will be a Grandmaster*. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

**Is my training progress backed up?**
Progress is stored only on your device. It is not synced between devices.
